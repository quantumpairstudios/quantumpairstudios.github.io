Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Sketch Bird is a game made for kids and families by Quantum Pair Studios. We don't ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location, and you never need an account to play.

📱 What stays on your device

Your game progress (level, coins, items, settings and language) is saved only on your device. We don't upload it anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

📺 Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward.
  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed. Ads are not personalized and are limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G).
  • The app does not use the device advertising ID.
  • Google may process limited technical data (such as the device type and IP address) to deliver ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses information in ads.

🛒 Purchases (Google Play)

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, feathers, hearts and Remove Ads) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details.

📊 Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We collect anonymous gameplay events (for example "level 5 completed" or "store opened") to see which parts of the game are fun and which are too hard. These events don't include names, emails or other personal details, and they aren't used for advertising.

👨‍👩‍👧 Children

Sketch Bird is designed to follow Google Play's Families Policy. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's personal information was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

✏️ Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
