Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026 · לגרסה בעברית

Sketch Bird is a game for kids and families made by Quantum Pair Studios, an independent developer in Israel. We don't ask for a name, email, photos, contacts or location, and nobody needs an account to play.

The app still sends some technical data to Google so it can show ads and so we can see how the game is played. This page explains exactly what that data is, who gets it and why.

📱 What stays on your device

Game progress (level, coins, items, settings and language) is saved only on the device. We don't upload it anywhere, and deleting the app deletes it.

📋 What the app collects, who gets it and why

There is no legal duty to give us any of this information. It is collected automatically while the app is used, and the game needs no other details from anyone.

  • Ads (Google AdMob): to show and count ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google receives technical data such as the device type, operating system, language and IP address. The recipient is Google.
  • Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): to learn which levels are too hard and which parts of the game are fun, the app sends gameplay events (for example "level 5 completed" or "store opened"), together with a random ID that Firebase creates for this installation of the app, the device type, the app version and the IP address, which Google uses to estimate the country. The recipient is Google.
  • Purchases (Google Play): to complete an optional purchase, Google Play handles the payment and tells the app which item was bought. The recipient is Google. We never see or store payment details.

We don't sell this information, and we don't pass it to anyone except Google as described above.

📺 Ads for kids

  • Some ads are optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward.
  • All ad requests are marked as child-directed. Ads are not personalized and are limited to content suitable for all ages (rated G).
  • The app does not use the device advertising ID, and game statistics are never used for advertising.
  • More about Google: how Google uses information in ads and Google's privacy policy.

🛒 Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (coin packs, feathers, a hearts refill and Remove Ads) go through Google Play, which asks the account owner to confirm each purchase. Everything else in the game's store is bought with coins and feathers earned by playing.

👨‍👩‍👧 Children and parents

Sketch Bird is made for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We collect only the technical data described above, never a child's name, contact details or photos, and nothing is used to build an advertising profile. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to see or delete the statistics linked to their child's device.

🌍 Storage outside Israel

Google processes this data on its servers, which are mostly outside Israel, including in the United States. Google handles it under its data processing terms, which include security and confidentiality commitments.

⏳ How long data is kept

Detailed game statistics are kept in Firebase for 2 months and then deleted automatically. Totals that can't be linked to a device (for example "how many players finished level 5") may be kept longer. Google's own retention of ad data is described in Google's privacy policy.

✋ Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you or your child, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us from the address below, and we will answer within 30 days. To find a device's statistics we may ask for a few details, such as roughly when the app was installed, but never for an ID document.

🌐 The website version

The free web version of the game shows no ads, offers no purchases and doesn't collect statistics. Progress is saved in your browser only.

✏️ Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If we start using information in a new way, we will tell players in the app first.
